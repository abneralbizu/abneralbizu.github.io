About

I spent four years in the U.S. Army as a Human Resources Specialist at Fort Bragg, where the work was records accuracy and regulatory compliance at scale — auditing personnel records against Army requirements, running inspections from Group to Battalion level, writing the standing procedures that governed recurring operations, and briefing what I found to the Company Commander and First Sergeant.

That turns out to be most of what security governance is: check the thing against the standard, document what you found, tell someone who can act on it, and write the procedure so the next person does it the same way. The technology was new to me. The discipline was not.

I hold a B.S. in Cybersecurity and an A.S. in Information Technology from Full Sail University, and I’m completing a second bachelor’s in Information Technology. I work as an IT and technology support specialist on contract, and I read, write and speak English and Spanish fluently.
